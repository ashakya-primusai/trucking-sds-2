Hi!

Enrout Ops is an AI dispatch platform for trucking teams — leads, loads, drivers, messages and tracking on one screen.

HOW TO SIGN IN
1. Open the Enrout Ops login page
2. Enter your work email address, then tap Send OTP
3. Check your inbox for a 6-digit code
4. Enter the code, then tap Verify & sign in
5. You land on the Dashboard — your home screen

SIDEBAR — WHERE EVERYTHING LIVES
- Dashboard — KPIs, critical messages, pipeline summary
- Leads — sales pipeline (negotiate, approve, promote)
- Load Management — all active loads, docs and actions
- Load Tracking — map, route and stop progress
- Communication — email, broker and driver WhatsApp in one inbox
- P&D Updates — pickup/delivery ETAs and delays
- Driver Management — add and edit your driver roster
- Configuration — company, fleet, drivers and broker setup
- Create Lead / Create Load — wizards to enter freight
- Assign Load — match loads to available drivers

FIRST-TIME SETUP (DO THIS ONCE)
Sidebar → Configuration
Work through each section and tap Save & Continue:
1. Company Info — business details and fleet lot address
2. Fleet — trucks and trailer types
3. Drivers — your roster (needed before assignment)
4. Broker Emails — inbound broker addresses
5. Documents — company compliance files
6. AI Automation — how Bella handles email and chat

Then: Sidebar → Driver Management → confirm every active driver is listed.

CREATE A LOAD (CONFIRMED FREIGHT)
Sidebar → Create Load
- Step 1 — Load Info: customer, source (broker, load board, etc.), priority and description
- Step 2 — Route & Stops: pickup and delivery addresses, dates and times
- Step 3 — Package Details: commodity, weight, trailer type, special requirements
- Step 4 — Driver & Cost: rate, fuel surcharge and charges, then submit
Load appears in Load Management.

CREATE A LEAD (OPPORTUNITY TO NEGOTIATE)
Sidebar → Create Lead — same 4-step wizard
- Pick the current lifecycle stage — earlier stages auto-complete
- No driver assignment on a lead
- When Approved → open in Leads → tap Promote to Load

ASSIGN A DRIVER
Sidebar → Assign Load
- Select an eligible load on the left (docs uploaded, pickup still in the future)
- Review ranked drivers on the right (schedule fit and travel time)
- Tap to assign, then track in Load Tracking and P&D Updates

DAILY ROUTINE
1. Dashboard — check critical messages and pipeline
2. Leads or Load Management — work your freight
3. Assign Load — match drivers to ready loads
4. P&D Updates — watch ETAs and delays (tap a card to open Communication)
5. Communication — reply to brokers and drivers (AI-suggested replies available)
6. Ask Bella on the Dashboard for load summaries, delays and stats

You stay in control — AI helps with follow-ups and replies. You approve rates, assign drivers and close loads.
