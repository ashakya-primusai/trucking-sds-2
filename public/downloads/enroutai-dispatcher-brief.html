First-time setup

Sidebar → Configuration — complete each section, click Save & Continue:

  1. Company Info — business & fleet lot
  2. Fleet — trucks & trailers
  3. Drivers — roster (required)
  1. Broker Emails — inbound offers
  2. Documents — compliance files
  3. AI Automation — Bella rules

Then confirm drivers in Driver Management.

Workflows & daily use

Lead-to-delivery flow

Create Lead → Negotiate → Approve → Promote to Load → Upload Docs → Assign Driver → Track P&D → Close

Manage leads

Sidebar → Leads — filter by stage, priority, source. Click a row for details, chat, activity, financials.

When Approved or Agreement Pending → Promote to Load. Stage becomes Docs Pending.

Load management

Sidebar → Load Management — click a row for the detail panel:

  • Documents — upload rate cons, BOL
  • Route / Financials — full load info
  • Chat & Activity — messages & history
  • Notify driver — WhatsApp from panel
  • Close load — mark complete

Assign a driver

Sidebar → Assign Load — load on left, ranked drivers on right.

Eligible loads:

  • Stage: Docs Pending, Border Approval, or Ready to Assign
  • No driver assigned; pickup still in the future

Drivers ranked by: schedule fit, travel time from last stop. Off-duty excluded.

Tracking & communication

  • Load Tracking — map, route, stop progress
  • P&D Updates — ETAs, delays; click card → Communication
  • Communication — email, WhatsApp, Bella; AI replies; mark resolved
  • Recurring Loads — templates for repeat lanes

Create freight: Create Lead (negotiate) or Create Load (confirmed)

Assign driver: Assign Load after docs uploaded

Repeat lanes: Recurring Loads templates

You stay in control — AI suggests and automates repetitive work. You approve rates, choose drivers, assign loads, and close freight.
