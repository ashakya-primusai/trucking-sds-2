Hi! 👋

*Enrout Ops* is an AI dispatch platform for trucking teams — leads, loads, drivers, messages & tracking on one screen.

*How to sign in:*
1. Open the Enrout Ops login page
2. Enter your work *email address* → tap *Send OTP*
3. Check your inbox for a *6-digit code*
4. Enter the code → tap *Verify & sign in*
5. You land on the *Dashboard* — your home screen

*Sidebar — where everything lives:*
• *Dashboard* — KPIs, critical messages, pipeline summary
• *Leads* — sales pipeline (negotiate → approve → promote)
• *Load Management* — all active loads, docs & actions
• *Load Tracking* — map, route & stop progress
• *Communication* — email, broker & driver WhatsApp in one inbox
• *P&D Updates* — pickup/delivery ETAs & delays
• *Driver Management* — add & edit your driver roster
• *Configuration* — company, fleet, drivers & broker setup
• *Create Lead* / *Create Load* — wizards to enter freight
• *Assign Load* — match loads to available drivers

*First-time setup (do this once):*
Sidebar → *Configuration*
Work through each section and tap *Save & Continue*:
1. *Company Info* — business details & fleet lot address
2. *Fleet* — trucks & trailer types
3. *Drivers* — your roster (needed before assignment)
4. *Broker Emails* — inbound broker addresses
5. *Documents* — company compliance files
6. *AI Automation* — how Bella handles email & chat

Then: Sidebar → *Driver Management* → confirm every active driver is listed.

*Create a load (confirmed freight):*
Sidebar → *Create Load*
• *Step 1 — Load Info:* customer, source (broker, load board, etc.), priority & description
• *Step 2 — Route & Stops:* pickup & delivery addresses, dates & times
• *Step 3 — Package Details:* commodity, weight, trailer type, special requirements
• *Step 4 — Driver & Cost:* rate, fuel surcharge & charges → submit
Load appears in *Load Management*

*Create a lead (opportunity to negotiate):*
Sidebar → *Create Lead* — same 4-step wizard
• Pick the current *lifecycle stage* — earlier stages auto-complete
• No driver assignment on a lead
• When *Approved* → open in *Leads* → tap *Promote to Load*

*Assign a driver:*
Sidebar → *Assign Load*
• Select an eligible load on the left (docs uploaded, pickup still in the future)
• Review ranked drivers on the right (schedule fit + travel time)
• Tap to assign → track in *Load Tracking* & *P&D Updates*

*Daily routine:*
1. *Dashboard* — check critical messages & pipeline
2. *Leads* or *Load Management* — work your freight
3. *Assign Load* — match drivers to ready loads
4. *P&D Updates* — watch ETAs & delays (tap a card → opens *Communication*)
5. *Communication* — reply to brokers & drivers (AI-suggested replies available)
6. Ask *Bella* on the Dashboard for load summaries, delays & stats

*You stay in control* — AI helps with follow-ups & replies. You approve rates, assign drivers & close loads.
